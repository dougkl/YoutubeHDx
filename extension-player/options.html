YoutubeHDx Player

Applies to every video you watch on youtube.com in this browser.

Playback quality

If a video doesn't go that high, the best quality below it is used.

Captions

If a video only has auto-generated English captions, captions stay off.

Looked up on LRCLIB (free) by artist and song from the video title, and shown only when the song's length matches the video, so the timing lines up. Only the title and length are sent.

Get a key from your OpenSubtitles account (opensubtitles.com/consumers). Free accounts have a small daily download limit. The key stays on this PC.

Subtitles out of sync on a video? Use the ⇄ button in the player's control bar to try other subtitles, nudge lyric timing, or turn them off for that video. Those choices are remembered per video on this PC.

TIDAL audio for music videos

Uses TIDAL's official player with your TIDAL account; nothing is downloaded or recorded. TIDAL only gives apps like this one 30-second previews, so full songs play in a TIDAL tab (tidal.com, signed in as you), pinned in the background and kept in step with the video. Chrome lets a tab play sound only after you've clicked in it once: the first time in each Chrome session, click Play once in that TIDAL tab. TIDAL plays on one device at a time. Switch back for one video with the ⇄ button or YouTube's right-click menu.
